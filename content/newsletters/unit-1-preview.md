A small taste of what 8th grade's Mad Libs generator looks like under the hood — this is genuinely most of the program:

```python
noun = input("Give me a noun: ")
adjective = input("Give me an adjective: ")
verb = input("Give me a verb: ")

print(f"The {adjective} {noun} decided to {verb} across the cafeteria.")
```

Three `input()` calls and one sentence template — and yet every student's version comes out different. That's the whole unit: variables and user input, doing real work.
