The single most common reason an assignment shows up as "missing" isn't that the work wasn't done — it's that it was attached but never actually turned in. Those are two different steps. Here's how to make sure yours counts.

![Illustration of a person checking off a task on a to-do list](https://cdn.undraw.co/illustration/add-tasks_mvlb.svg)

## Step 1: Open the assignment

Go to the **Classwork** tab in your class, find the assignment, and click it. This opens the assignment's detail page, where you can see the instructions, due date, and point value.

## Step 2: Attach or create your work

On the right side of the assignment page, click **Add or create**. From there you can:

- Upload a file from your computer
- Attach something already in your Google Drive
- Create a new Doc, Slides, or Sheet directly from the assignment

At this point, your file is attached to the assignment — but it is **not submitted yet**.

## Step 3: Click "Turn In"

This is the step that gets skipped. After attaching your file, click the blue **Turn in** button (or **Mark as done** for assignments that don't require a file). The button will change to **Turned in**, and that status is what your teacher actually sees.

## Step 4: Double-check the status

Before you close the tab, confirm the assignment shows **Turned in**, not just a file sitting in the attachments area. If you need to fix something after submitting, click **Unsubmit**, make your changes, and turn it in again — just watch the due date if you're close to it.

## What the statuses mean

- **Assigned** — you haven't opened or started it yet
- **Turned in** — submitted and waiting to be graded
- **Missing** — the due date passed with nothing turned in
- **Late** — turned in, but after the due date
- **Returned** — your teacher has graded it and sent feedback back

## The one-sentence version

Attaching a file shows your work to the assignment page; clicking **Turn in** is what actually tells your teacher you're done — do both, every time.
